# Bonsai interface direction

User preference, 9 October 2026: a visually appealing, feature-packed minimalist app.

- Lists inherit the entire page background. Use color changes for hover and meaningful selection only.
- Put compact facts beside the data or inside cards. Avoid page-end disclaimer paragraphs, duplicate controls, technical explanations and source-setting disclosures in normal feature screens.
- Keep network-source choices in Settings. Show the update source beside each app.
- Available-update rows obey the same sort selection and direction as the installed-app list.
- Startup rows use an app name, concise App subtitle and a state control. Technical ownership and saved-state details can remain in accessibility/help or audit records.
- Use one clear action for routine operations and reversible settings. Keep concise confirmation for the reviewed personal files being removed, irreversible deletion and genuinely required macOS authorization.
- Actual failures and missing access need short, actionable states. Do not silently present incomplete measurements or unavailable readings as complete.
- Analyze starts with the realistic black pine and Map your disk / Choose folder, then shows the map with its side tree.
- Golden ginkgo is the identity icon. Cherry blossom, elm, maple, pine and olive belong to their respective pages.
